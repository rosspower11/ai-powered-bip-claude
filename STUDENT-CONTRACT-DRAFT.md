## Notes for the legal/Claude pass

1. **Governing law** (§12) — pick a jurisdiction. UAE / DIFC, England & Wales, or Dubai courts are the obvious options for a Dubai-registered company; choose based on where your customers are and where you want disputes resolved.
2. **VAT / tax** — fees are now generic (set at enrolment) rather than stated in the contract, but the entity is UAE-registered (5% VAT). Confirm whether the L.L.C-FZ is VAT-registered and whether VAT applies to digital education sold to non-UAE customers.
3. **GDPR / data protection** — recordings include personal data of EU/UK students. Worth a short data-protection clause covering: where recordings are stored, how long, who can access, the Student's right to request deletion.
4. **Distance-selling refund window** — UK consumers buying digital goods have a statutory 14-day cooling-off period unless they waive it once delivery starts. I've used 7 days as a sensible default but check this against the markets you sell to.
5. **"Materially shortens"** in §9 — loose phrasing; tighten in legal pass.
6. **Office Hours classification** — currently labelled *optional* in §2 to match the landing page. If you want students contractually expected to attend, change the wording.
7. **Image / likeness usage** — separate from recordings shared within the cohort. If you intend to use Student footage for marketing (testimonials, social posts), add an explicit clause and consent box.
